# Ablation & realizability analysis

Supplementary experiments over the conjecture campaign:
- `e4_feedback_ablation.py` — outcome-signal (feedback) ablation of the generator.
- `reachability.py` — per-track provenance and plantri-reachability of refutation witnesses.
- `simple_baseline.py` — the "enumerate realizable p-vectors + fit linear bounds" baseline.
- `trusted_base.py` / `proof_deps.py` — per-theorem dependency on the sorry-marked inventory base.
- `funnel.py`, `families.py`, `provenance.py`, `residual.py` — campaign funnel and family analyses.

Outputs are written under `out/`.
